A little clarity

Before we begin.

Which package should I choose?

Use the project planner to outline your goals. Standard starts at ₹4,999, Classic at ₹6,999, Premium at ₹8,999 and Business at ₹11,999. Custom features are quoted separately.

Are renewals and revisions included?

Website packages include one year of domain and hosting. Renewal charges, revision rounds, maintenance, delivery dates and applicable taxes are confirmed in the written quotation.

Can you integrate booking, online payments or business tools?

We can discuss the required integration and create a separate scope. Provider accounts, transaction charges, subscriptions and approvals depend on the service selected.

Is the studio assistant a live AI agent?

The website assistant uses a curated local knowledge base for fast answers. It does not browse the web or represent a human. Use its WhatsApp handoff to speak with the studio.
